Privacy Policy for YTfy

Last Updated: {{ last_updated }}

Overview

YTfy is a bidirectional playlist converter that transfers playlists between YouTube and Spotify. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We do NOT store your YouTube or Spotify playlist URLs
  • ❌ We do NOT save your Spotify or YouTube account information
  • ❌ We do NOT keep any database of user data
  • ❌ We do NOT track your browsing habits
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your information with third parties
  • ❌ We do NOT use cookies for tracking (only session cookies for authentication)

What We Do

  • ✅ We temporarily access your YouTube playlists to read song titles (including private playlists with your permission)
  • ✅ We temporarily access your Spotify playlists to read song titles (including private playlists with your permission)
  • ✅ We create or modify playlists on Spotify and YouTube (with your permission)
  • ✅ All processing happens in real-time and nothing is stored after conversion

Third-Party Services

YTfy uses the following services:

YouTube Data API

  • Used to: Read and create playlist information
  • Data accessed: Playlist titles, song titles, and playlist management (with your permission)
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

Spotify Web API

  • Used to: Read and create playlists and add songs to your account
  • Data accessed: Your Spotify user ID, playlists (including private ones), and song information (with your explicit permission)
  • Privacy Policy: Spotify Privacy Policy

Permissions We Request

Spotify Permissions

  • playlist-modify-public: To create or modify public playlists
  • playlist-modify-private: To create or modify private playlists
  • playlist-read-private: To read your private playlists for conversion

YouTube Permissions

  • youtube: To manage your YouTube playlists and read private playlists

These permissions are ONLY used for playlist conversion and nothing else.

Data Retention

  • Session Data: Temporary session data (like authentication tokens) is stored in your browser session and deleted when you close your browser or log out
  • No Permanent Storage: We do not maintain any servers or databases that store your information

Your Rights

You have full control:

  • Revoke YTfy's access to Spotify anytime through your Spotify Account Settings
  • Revoke YTfy's access to YouTube anytime through your Google Account Permissions
  • Delete any playlists created by YTfy directly in Spotify or YouTube
  • Disconnect individual accounts (Spotify or YouTube) independently from the app
  • Stop using YTfy anytime with no data left behind

Security

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • We follow Spotify and YouTube's security best practices
  • Authentication tokens are handled securely and never logged
  • Separate session management for Spotify and YouTube accounts

Children's Privacy

YTfy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
